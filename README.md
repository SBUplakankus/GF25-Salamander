# 🦎 Salamander

> *A short 3D single-player survival game with narrative elements*  
> 🏆 **Winner: Best in User Interface Design — Games Fleadh 2025**  
> Developed for the **Games Fleadh 2025** student competition under the theme **“Small World”**  
> Organized by *Technological University of the Shannon (TUS) Thurles, Ireland*

---

## 🌍 About the Game

**Salamander** is a short, atmospheric survival experience where you play as a **salamander** struggling to survive in a world threatened by **deforestation**.

Your goal is simple but challenging — **stay alive** by balancing three critical needs:

- 🍽️ **Hunger** – Consume food to stay fed  
- 💧 **Moisture** – Keep your body hydrated by taking dips in the water  
- 🐍 **Safety** – Defend yourself from **invasive predatory snakes** by:
  - Spitting to attack  
  - Dashing to escape danger  

> Are there any chances of survival — or are you doomed to fail?

---

## 🧠 Narrative Theme

The game highlights the **environmental impact of human interference**, showing how shrinking natural habitats endanger species that once thrived.  
Your “small world” becomes smaller still — until survival itself is uncertain.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay Summary

- Explore a lush but fragile **3D environment**  
- Balance survival mechanics (hunger, moisture, safety)  
- Engage in light **combat** and **avoidance** gameplay  
- Experience a **narrative-driven ending cutscene**  
- Average playtime: **5–10 minutes**
